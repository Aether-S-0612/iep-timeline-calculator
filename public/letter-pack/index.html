Letter pack · $12, one time

The letters to the school, already written with your dates

Knowing the deadline is one thing. Writing to the school about it, without sounding angry or getting the date wrong, is the part most parents put off. This pack writes those emails for you, with your child's dates and your state's rules already filled in.

Pick your state to start: New York · Pennsylvania · Illinois · California. Enter your consent date, and the letter appears right under your timeline. The first half is free to read.

What's in it

4 letters, one for each stage

  • Confirm the dates. A friendly note right after consent, so everyone works from the same deadline.
  • A date has passed. A polite follow-up that names the rule and asks when it will happen.
  • Still no reply. A second request to the superintendent, with the date of your first email.
  • After the meeting. A short summary of what was agreed, so it's in writing.

Sent from your own email

One click opens the letter in Gmail or your usual mail app, ready to send. It goes from you, so the dated record is in your own sent folder. We never send anything for you.

IEP meeting prep

What to bring, questions to ask, what to do the night before, and a few calm phrases for when the meeting moves too fast.

Plain-English help

The words you'll hear in the meeting, explained, and what to do step by step if the school stops answering.

Why each letter says it the way it does

Every letter is polite on purpose. It names the date and the regulation, asks one clear question, and leaves room for the school to fix it, which makes it easy for them to answer. Under each letter you'll see a one-line note on why it's worded the way it is.

Questions

The calculator, every date and rule, and the first half of each letter are free. The $12 unlocks the full letters, one-click sending, the meeting prep and the glossary.

No. It's general information and letter templates based on the published rules. For advice about your child's situation, your state's free Parent Center is a great place to start.
