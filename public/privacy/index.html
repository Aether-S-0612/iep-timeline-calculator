Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

The calculator

Everything you type into the calculator, including dates and school calendars, is processed in your own browser. It is not sent to or stored on our servers. The only exceptions are the optional email forms and the meeting practice tool described below.

What your browser stores

This site saves a few things in your own browser (localStorage): whether you unlocked the letter pack, the last calculator page you used, and the dates you entered so you don't have to type them again. They stay on your device. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages), which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operations. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Purchases

If you buy a product, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email and payment details to process the order and send your download. We receive your name, email and order details so we can provide support and refunds. We do not receive your full card number. When you unlock the letter pack, your license key is sent to Lemon Squeezy to check that it's valid. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.

Email list

If you sign up for the free checklist, your email address is stored by our email provider, MailerLite, so we can send it to you and, now and then, tell you about new guides and states. If you ask for a deadline reminder, MailerLite stores your email, your state, and the one date and step you picked, so we can send two emails about that date (3 days before and the day after). Every email has an unsubscribe link. See the MailerLite privacy policy.

Meeting practice

On the meeting practice page, what you type, plus the state and situation you picked, is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, so it can write the school's reply and your tip. We don't save the conversation; it stays in your browser tab until you close it. Anthropic may keep API data for a limited time under its own policy and does not use it to train its models by default. See the Anthropic privacy policy. Please don't type your child's name or other details that could identify them.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, and we do not ask for information about your child's disability or records.

Children

This site is for parents and school staff. It is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Do Not Track

Our site does not track you across other websites, and we do not use advertising cookies. We don't change how the site works based on a Do Not Track signal, because there is no cross-site tracking to turn off.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, including order details and your email list record. Email iepdeadlines@gmail.com. You can also unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you already gave us, we'll email you first.
