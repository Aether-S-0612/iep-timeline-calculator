Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

The calculator

Everything you type into the calculator, including dates and school calendars, is processed in your own browser. It is not sent to or stored on our servers.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operations. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Purchases

If you buy a product, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email and payment details to process the order and send your download. We receive your name, email and order details so we can provide support and refunds. We do not receive your full card number. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, and we do not ask for information about your child's disability or records.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the order information we hold about you. Email iepdeadlines@gmail.com.
