Refund Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

If the letter and meeting prep are not useful to you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase and you will get your money back in full. No explanation is needed. Refunds are limited to one per customer.

When a refund is issued, the license key for that purchase stops working.

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy. Depending on your bank, they usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

If you think a date we calculated is wrong, please tell us what you entered (state, consent date and school calendar). We check every report, whether or not you ask for a refund.
